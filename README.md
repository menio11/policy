# policy
This application uses your device location to provide weather forecasts along your travel route. Your location and destination are only used within the app to calculate routes and weather.

If you choose to open a route in Google Maps, Waze, or another navigation app, your location and destination may be shared with that app.

We use third-party advertising services to display ads. These services may collect limited device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and app usage data to serve relevant or non-personalized ads.

We do not store, track, or directly identify users using this information. No personal location history or route data is collected or stored by this app.
